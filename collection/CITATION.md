### Version DOI and concept DOI

A Zenodo **version DOI** identifies one archived release. Use that identifier when reporting a teaching intervention, calculation, comparison or other work that depends on the exact files.

The **concept DOI** represents the collection across its versions. It is suitable when referring to the evolving collection as a whole.
